About Me

Welcome to my website! I’m Amin, an engineer, researcher, and PhD candidate at TU/e. I enjoy understanding how complex systems work and using that understanding to solve practical problems. My research includes strategic decision making in the circular economy and remanufacturing, where I bring together engineering knowledge, mathematical modeling, and optimization to support decisions about recovering value from used products and components. My background in robotics, control and mechatronics has shaped how I approach these challenges. I combine advanced control, AI, and machine learning to develop systems that learn, adapt, and work together. In additive manufacturing, I work on planning and control to help processes meet their quality requirements. I also study battery modeling and state estimation, focusing on understanding battery behavior and estimating the remaining useful life of batteries. Alongside my research, my industry experience spans industrial automation, health care devices, predictive maintenance, and machine vision. I enjoy bringing hardware and software together, taking ideas from models and simulations through to working systems. Explore my projects and publications to learn more about my work and the questions I’m investigating.
